## Decision logic

- Auto-fix vs escalate boundary is the Shopify guardrail allow-list, nothing wider: only title, description, meta title, meta description, image alt, GTIN/MPN, google_product_category, and product_type are auto-applied. Price, inventory/availability, publish status, and bulk operations are ALWAYS recommend/escalate.
- Disapproval-risk copy: targeted compliant word-swaps are auto-fixed (anti-blemish -> 'for blemish-prone skin'; anti-ageing -> 'for mature / firming feel'; 'repairs impaired barrier' -> 'supports the skin barrier'; strip treats/cures/heals/prescription). BUT any CBD/hemp-suspect item (Moon Drops, Barrier Support/Combo) is ESCALATED for ingredient verification — copy edits alone cannot clear an ingredient-based drug flag, and 'when unsure, escalate'.
- GTIN rule: never invent an identifier. Apply a GTIN only from a verified barcode already on the product/packaging; for genuine no-GTIN owned SA brands set identifier_exists=false. A fabricated GTIN is worse than none.
- Meta Catalog is the mirror, Shopify is the source: because Content ID = Variant ID and the F&I channel keeps the catalog in sync, every Meta-side item error is fixed at the Shopify variant, never by writing to catalog 1823440805035326 directly (catalog writes are outside the Meta allow-list).
- Prioritisation: (1) drug/clinical/CBD disapprovals = account-suspension risk = top priority regardless of the product's revenue; (2) products currently taking Google Shopping / Advantage+ spend per the 01 PPC report — especially any over the CPA ceiling (R167 BoT, R300 general) — get feed fixes next; (3) bestsellers and Pastry SKUs (top revenue driver, 21 of top 145 bestsellers) before slow_mover / new_arrival.
- Title formula = Brand + Product + key attribute/ingredient + size, primary SA keyword (from Semrush) in the first 70 chars, <=150 total; meta title <=60 chars, brand + keyword. English only, never any free-shipping/free-delivery phrase, 'proudly South African' for Pastry/Mzuri/B'AiR/Lelive/Forme/Skin Functional.
- custom_label alignment (Simprosys, recommend-only): custom_label_0 margin tier (SA brands high, established K-beauty medium, premium imports low), _1 origin (sa_brand/k_beauty/international), _2 performance derived from ShopifyQL sell-through, _3 price band, _4 season/promo (SA seasons reversed: summer_spf Nov-Feb, winter_skin Jun-Aug). Recommended, not applied — no Simprosys API and Simprosys is a Do-Not-Touch app.
- google_product_category is set/corrected to the true Google beauty taxonomy node; product_type is kept coherent with the merchandising Good/Better/Best architecture so downstream Shopping/PMax bidding by product_type stays clean.
- Data honesty: all figures cited to a tool call, report line, or CSV row; anything from Merchant Center without a live export is tagged NOT VERIFIED; any data spanning Feb 23 is caveated and only post-Feb-23 figures are used (pre-block metrics are bot-inflated).
- If a finding is ambiguous (is 'firming' a claim? is this the correct taxonomy leaf?), do not guess-and-write — record it as a Recommendation and move on. Every applied change must be reversible with the logged revert step or it is not applied.

## Escalate — recommend only, never auto-done

- Google Merchant Center 5717759749 disapprovals (no API): produce a ready-to-action fix list with disapproval reason per SKU; drug / prescription / misleading-claims disapprovals flagged as account-SUSPENSION risk and top priority.
- CBD/hemp ingredient-suspect items (Moon Drops, Barrier Support / Barrier Combo): recommend ingredient verification + reformulated compliant copy — do NOT auto-edit; the flag is ingredient-based, not just copy.
- Price and availability/inventory mismatches between Shopify and the feed: recommend correction only (guardrails forbid changing price and inventory is outside the allow-list); confirm Simprosys out-of-stock exclusion is functioning.
- Products missing a usable primary image or carrying only lifestyle imagery: recommend sourcing/reordering a clean primary image (agent cannot generate product photography).
- Simprosys custom_label_0..4 rule updates (margin tier / origin / performance / price band / season): recommend the exact label values (custom_label_2 from ShopifyQL sell-through) for a human to set in the Simprosys dashboard — no Simprosys API and Simprosys is Do-Not-Touch.
- Google Ads Shopping feed exclusions or bid-by-attribute changes: recommend-only — deliver a Google Ads Editor CSV, step list, or Google Ads Script; never claim the account was changed; never fabricate account numbers.
- Branded products that require a GTIN but have none available: recommend a supplier/principal follow-up to obtain the barcode.
