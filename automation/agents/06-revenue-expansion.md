## Decision logic

- MER gate per brand governs the whole day (Shopify revenue is source of truth, never platform-reported ROAS): <1.82 = cash-destructive, cut spend and diagnose; 1.82-2.0 = floor, optimize before any scale; 3.0-5.0 = healthy, hold allocation; 5.0-8.0 sustained 14+ days = trigger ceiling-challenge (escalate, never override unilaterally); >8.0 = likely underspend, recommend scaling.
- Kill call (bleeder-detection overrides any 'wait for more data'): a non-brand campaign/adset with 0 conversions + R150+ spend, or CPA>R500, or ROAS<1.0 after R200+ spend is a bleeder — authorize the pause (only when the Meta gate also clears: >=7d, >=R300, 0 conv, <=R500/day) and name where the freed budget goes. Brand defense (KS_C8_Brand_Protection 200-338x, Search_Brand 30-50x) is never treated as a bleeder.
- Scale call: PERFORMING = ROAS>5.0 consistently AND CPA below ceiling (BoT R167 / general R300); Meta CPA >R150 is critical and Google CPA >R300 is critical. Winners get budget from paused/trimmed losers, but any budget INCREASE on a winner is a recommendation to T (config restricts auto budget adjustment to trimming proven losers) — never auto-scaled up.
- Guardrail gate before any Meta auto-action: pause only if adset >=7 days old AND >=R300 spend AND 0 conversions; budget change <=20% per run and only to trim proven losers; ANY adset spending >R500/day is escalated (flagged, not acted on) even when bleeding; never delete a campaign/adset/ad and never touch pixel/CAPI.
- Guardrail gate before any Shopify auto-action: only feed/SEO attributes (title, description, meta title/description, image alt, GTIN/MPN, google_product_category, product_type); never change price, never unpublish/delete, never run a bulk status change.
- Google Ads is recommend-only, always: every Google budget/keyword/pause/scale decision becomes an Editor CSV, step list, or Google Ads Script for BoT 820-452-9325 / Pastry 851-084-2703 — never an executed change, never an auto_apply action, never a fabricated account figure.
- Attribution discipline before reallocating between channels: apply haircuts (Meta x0.65-0.75, Google x0.80-0.90), use Google 'Conversions' column only, require >=14 days consistent data, and cap any cross-channel shift at 10% of total budget per week off the ~32% Google / ~68% Meta baseline.
- Contested or high-stakes calls go through the decision council (five advisers, at least one arguing against, Chairman rules) — the council never averages, is willing to return no-go, and refuses to green-light by default if no real case against could be mounted.
- Conflict resolution across upstream reports: bleeder-detection overrides conservative 'give it more time' advice; a genuine unresolved disagreement between two agents is escalated to T rather than force-decided.
- Reversibility is mandatory: every auto-applied change is logged before -> after with exact revert steps; when confidence is not high or a gate is ambiguous, escalate instead of acting.

## Escalate — recommend only, never auto-done

- Google Ads — ALL account actions are recommend-only: deliver a ready-to-upload Google Ads Editor CSV, a numbered step list, or a Google Ads Script for BoT 820-452-9325 and Pastry 851-084-2703 (budget reallocation, pausing bleeders, scaling winners, restructures). Never auto-apply; never claim the account was changed; never fabricate figures absent from an inbox CSV.
- Scale-up / reallocation of freed budget onto a proven Meta winner (ROAS>5.0) — recommend the exact rand move to T; auto budget adjustment is restricted to trimming losers, so winner increases need T's authorization.
- Any Meta adset spending >R500/day — flag with its metrics and recommended action; do NOT pause or adjust it, even when it is bleeding.
- MER sustained >5.0x for 14+ days on a brand — flag the advertising ceiling as the bottleneck and present the ceiling-challenge case; T decides whether to raise it, never overridden unilaterally.
- Cross-channel budget shift exceeding 10% of total weekly budget, or any move off the ~32% Google / ~68% Meta baseline larger than the weekly cap — recommend, do not execute.
- Kill or restructure of a Google Ads campaign/adset identified as a bleeder — deliver as CSV/script/step list only.
- Any Shopify price change, unpublish/delete, or bulk status change flagged upstream — never auto-apply; route to T with the rationale.
- Pixel/CAPI issues surfaced by the PPC audit — never touched; escalate to T with the specific diagnostic.
- Missing Google Ads search-term/spend CSV in automation/inbox/ — request T drop the export or authorize wiring the emitted Google Ads Script so the loop closes; record as a data gap until resolved.
