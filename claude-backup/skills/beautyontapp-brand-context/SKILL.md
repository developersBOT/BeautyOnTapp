# Brand Marketing Context — PNCapital Portfolio

Read this skill FIRST before any marketing, content, copywriting, or customer-facing task. Load the brand-specific reference file for the brand being discussed.

## HARD RULES
1. Never mix brand voices. Each brand has a distinct personality.
2. SA brands = "proudly South African." Never "in-house" or "our own brand."
3. English only. 🖤 only for BeautyOnTApp.
4. "Same-day delivery" in customer-facing content. Never "1-hour delivery."

## SWITCHING DYNAMICS (JTBD Four Forces)

**Push** (frustrations driving them away from alternatives):
- International brands not formulated for African climate/melanin-rich skin
- No local stores to test products — online-only risk
- High import prices, slow international shipping (2-4 weeks)
- Generic routines ignoring SA-specific skin concerns (hyperpigmentation #1)

**Pull** (what attracts to BeautyOnTApp):
- Test in 6 physical stores before buying
- Same-day delivery in JHB metro
- Expert skin analysis (R285) with trained beauty advisors
- 80+ curated brands — K-beauty + SA brands in one destination
- Content in SA context (climate, ingredients, pricing in ZAR)

**Habit** (what keeps them stuck with alternatives):
- Brand loyalty to The Ordinary, CeraVe (available at Clicks/Dis-Chem)
- Clicks ClubCard and Dis-Chem loyalty points
- Takealot convenience and TakealotMORE free delivery
- Woolworths premium trust and credit account

**Anxiety** (worries about switching):
- "Will K-beauty work for my skin?" — addressed by skin analysis + education content
- "Is it worth the price?" — addressed by routine builders showing cost-per-day
- "Can I trust an online-only brand?" — addressed by 6 physical stores

## CUSTOMER LANGUAGE (SA Beauty)

**Words customers use:**
- "Glow" (not "radiance"), "dark spots" (not "hyperpigmentation" in ads), "oily T-zone," "dry patches," "breakouts" (not "acne" in Meta ads — policy risk), "routine," "glass skin," "my skin is popping"

**Words to avoid in customer-facing content:**
- "Acne" in Meta/Google ad copy (disapproval risk — use "blemish-prone")
- "Anti-aging" (use "age-preventative" or "youthful skin")
- "Whitening/lightening" (use "brightening" or "even-toned") — SAHPRA + cultural sensitivity
- "Cheap" (use "affordable" or "accessible")
