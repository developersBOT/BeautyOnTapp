## NEW BRAND EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

### The 7-Gate Assessment
Before adding ANY new brand, score against these 7 criteria:

**Gate 1 — Market Demand (Weight: 25%)**
- Is there SA search volume for this brand? (Check Google Trends, keyword tools)
- Is it trending on SA TikTok/Instagram? (Social listening)
- Are customers asking for it? (Chatbot queries, in-store requests)
- Is it on competitors' shelves? (Secret Skin, Glow Theory, Clicks, Woolworths)

**Gate 2 — Margin Viability (Weight: 20%)**
- Wholesale cost vs recommended retail → gross margin 55%+ minimum
- Landed cost after import (1.3-1.6× FOB) still delivers target margin?
- MAP enforcement — will they protect your pricing from Takealot/marketplace undercutting?
- Payment terms — can you get NET30+ or only prepay?

**Gate 3 — Brand Strength (Weight: 15%)**
- Social media following (global + SA-specific)
- Press/editorial coverage
- Awards or certifications (e.g., Olive Young Awards, K-beauty awards)
- Brand storytelling quality — does it have a compelling origin story?

**Gate 4 — SA Market Fit (Weight: 15%)**
- Products suitable for melanin-rich skin? (shade ranges, no-white-cast SPF, hyperpigmentation solutions)
- Ingredients tested on diverse skin tones?
- Price point appropriate for SA market? (adjust for Rand)
- Climate suitability? (humidity-proof, SPF adequate for SA UV index)

**Gate 5 — Competitive Advantage (Weight: 10%)**
- Can we get SA exclusivity or first-to-market?
- Does this brand fill a gap no competitor covers?
- Does it strengthen a specific skin concern category we want to own?

**Gate 6 — Supply Chain Reliability (Weight: 10%)**
- MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) manageable? (ideally $1,000-$5,000 for initial order)
- Lead time acceptable? (8-12 weeks sea freight is standard)
- Consistent stock availability — do they frequently run out of hero products?
- English labeling available or do we need overlays?

**Gate 7 — Cannibalisation Risk (Weight: 5%)**
- Does this brand overlap heavily with existing brands?
- Will it steal sales from Pastry Skincare? (protect the profit engine)
- Does adding it dilute curation strength or strengthen it?

### Scoring
Rate each gate 1-5. Weighted score > 3.5 = proceed. 3.0-3.5 = conditional (test with small order). Below 3.0 = pass.

## DISTRIBUTION AGREEMENT NEGOTIATION

### What to Negotiate
| Term | Ideal | Acceptable | Walk Away |
|---|---|---|---|
| SA exclusivity | Exclusive stockist for SA | First-to-market (6-month lead) | No exclusivity at all |
| MAP enforcement | Written MAP agreement | Informal price floor commitment | No pricing control |
| Payment terms | NET 60 | NET 30 | 100% prepay on ongoing orders |
| MOQ | $1,000-$3,000 first order | $3,000-$5,000 | >$10,000 first order |
| Return/exchange | Damaged/expired product return | Credit for next order | No returns policy |
| Marketing support | Co-op fund (3-5% of purchases) | Free samples + imagery assets | No support |
| Testers/samples | Free testers for 6 stores | Testers at cost | No testers available |
| Shelf life on delivery | 18+ months remaining | 12+ months | <12 months |

### SA Exclusivity is the #1 Priority
If Woolworths adds K-beauty brands, exclusive distribution agreements are the ONLY defence. Approach Korean brand principals (not distributors) with:
- "We're SA's leading K-beauty specialist with 6 premium mall locations"
- "We offer skin analysis (R285) that drives informed purchases, not impulse"
- "We'll build brand-specific content, SEO, and social presence in SA"
- "We'll provide monthly sell-through data and customer feedback"

## PRODUCT LIFECYCLE MANAGEMENT

### 4-Phase Lifecycle
**Phase 1 — Launch (Months 1-3)**
- Feature in "New Arrivals" collection
- Blog post / brand spotlight (see seo-content skill)
- Social media introduction
- In-store sampling / tester placement
- Staff training on the brand (see staff-training skill)
- Track: units sold, sell-through rate, customer feedback

**Phase 2 — Growth (Months 3-12)**
- Move top performers to "Best Sellers" / hero collections
- Include in routine bundles
- Expand range (add complementary products from same brand)
- Negotiate better terms based on sell-through data
- Track: repeat purchase rate, margin contribution, AOV impact

**Phase 3 — Maturity (Months 12-24)**
- Optimise reorder quantities based on historical data
- Include in loyalty promotions and GWP
- Cross-sell with newer brands
- Monitor for decline signals (slowing sell-through, fewer customer inquiries)

**Phase 4 — Decline / Exit**
- Sell-through rate below 20% for 3+ months → reduce reorder
- No customer inquiries for 60+ days → mark for clearance
- **Clearance options:** In-store markdown (20-30% off), bundle with high-performers, Takealot marketplace (ONLY for discontinued lines — see takealot-intel decision rules)
- NEVER let dead stock expire on shelf — beauty products have finite shelf life

### Expiry Date Management
- K-beauty products: typically 24-36 month total shelf life from manufacture
- Target minimum 18 months remaining on delivery
- Track expiry in Shopify via tags or custom metafields
- Products within 6 months of expiry → priority sell-through (GWP, bundles)
- Products within 3 months → clearance pricing or staff purchase at cost
- Products past expiry → destroy (NEVER sell expired cosmetics — CPA violation)

## DEAD STOCK & INVENTORY HEALTH

### Key Metrics
| Metric | Target | Action if Missed |
|---|---|---|
| Inventory turnover | 4-6× per year | Review underperformers quarterly |
| Sell-through rate | 60%+ within 6 months | Products below 30% → clearance consideration |
| Dead stock (no sales in 90 days) | <5% of total SKUs | Evaluate: bundle, GWP, clearance, or delist |
| Expiry risk (within 6 months) | <3% of inventory value | Prioritise in promotions and GWP |

### Preventing Dead Stock
1. Start with small test orders for new brands (MOQ minimum)
2. Use Shopify pre-orders for unproven products
3. Monitor weekly sell-through by brand, not just category
4. Cross-reference social buzz with actual sales — TikTok virality doesn't always convert in SA
5. Staff can be the early warning system — track what customers ask about but DON'T buy

## DECISION RULES

1. **When evaluating a new brand:** Run the 7-Gate Assessment. Score > 3.5 = go. 3.0-3.5 = small test order. Below 3.0 = pass.
2. **When a brand overlaps with Pastry Skincare:** PROTECT the profit engine. Only add if it serves a clearly different customer need or price point.
3. **When negotiating with Korean principals:** Lead with SA exclusivity request. BeautyOnTApp's 6 physical stores + skin analysis is a unique value proposition that online-only competitors can't offer.
4. **When a product isn't selling:** 90 days with <30% sell-through → bundle. 180 days → clearance. Approaching expiry → GWP or staff purchase. NEVER let product expire on shelf.
5. **When Woolworths or Clicks adds a K-beauty brand you carry:** This validates your curation but increases competition. Ensure you have exclusive SKUs, better education content, and stronger staff knowledge than they can deploy.
6. **When trending products go viral on TikTok:** Don't panic-buy. Check if trend translates to SA market. Order conservatively. Let online pre-order gauge demand before committing to 6-store stock.
7. **When planning seasonal assortment:** SA seasons are REVERSED from Northern Hemisphere. Summer = Nov-Feb (SPF, lightweight), Winter = Jun-Aug (rich moisturisers, barrier repair).
