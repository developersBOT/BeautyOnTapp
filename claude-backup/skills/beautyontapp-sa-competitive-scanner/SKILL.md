## COMPETITIVE SCAN FRAMEWORK

When T asks "what are they doing?" or requests a competitive check, follow this framework:

### Quick Scan (Single Competitor)
1. **Web search** the competitor name + "South Africa" + current year
2. **Check**: Store count, product range, pricing on 3 hero products, any new campaigns, delivery terms
3. **Compare**: How does this affect BeautyOnTApp's positioning?
4. **Action**: One specific recommendation based on the finding

### Full Market Scan (All Competitors)
1. Search each Tier 1 and Tier 2 competitor
2. Build comparison table: pricing, range, delivery, unique selling points
3. Identify: Where is BeautyOnTApp winning? Where is BeautyOnTApp losing?
4. Apply Porter's Five Forces from hbs-strategy skill
5. Deliver 3 actionable recommendations

### Price Comparison Protocol
When T asks about competitor pricing:
1. Search for the EXACT same product (brand + product name + size)
2. Note: Our price, their price, delivery cost, total cost to customer
3. Flag if competitor offers free delivery (we NEVER will — but customer comparison matters)
4. Recommend: adjust product price, add bundle value, or emphasize non-price differentiators

## DECISION RULES

1. **When a competitor launches K-beauty**: Check which brands they carry. If they have a brand we don't, evaluate adding it. If they're undercutting on price, DON'T race to the bottom — emphasize service, expertise, and in-store experience.
2. **When Clicks/Dis-Chem expands beauty**: This validates the market. Use their marketing spend as free awareness-building, then capture the educated customer with specialist expertise.
3. **When an international competitor enters SA**: Check their SA delivery cost + time. Usually 10-21 days + duties. Our 1-4 day delivery is the moat.
4. **When a competitor runs a sale**: Do NOT match blindly. Check if it's clearance (dead stock) or strategic. Only match if it's a hero product we both carry and the margin allows it.
5. **When T asks "should we worry about X?"**: Scan the competitor, assess their trajectory, and give a CLEAR verdict: "Real threat — here's why and what to do" or "Not a threat right now — here's why, but monitor."
