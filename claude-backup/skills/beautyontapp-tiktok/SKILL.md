## CONTENT FORMAT GUIDELINES

| Format | Length | When to Use |
|---|---|---|
| Quick demo/tutorial | 15-30 seconds | Product demos, ingredient explainers, "did you know" |
| Routine video | 30-60 seconds | Full routine tutorials, morning/night routines |
| Story/reaction | 15-45 seconds | Trend-jacking, product reactions, hauls |
| Educational deep-dive | 60-90 seconds | Ingredient science, K-beauty philosophy, skin concern explainers |

### Production Rules
- **UGC aesthetic > polished production.** TikTok rewards authenticity. Phone camera, natural lighting, real skin.
- **Hook in first 2 seconds.** "Your dark spots will hate this" > "Today I'm going to show you a product"
- **Text overlays on every video.** 80%+ of TikTok is watched without sound.
- **Face on camera performs 2-3× better** than product-only shots.
- **Vertical only.** 9:16 ratio. Never landscape.
- **Post 4-7× per week.** Consistency beats virality. Volume = discovery on TikTok's algorithm.

## FAILED APPROACHES — Do Not Repeat

| Approach | Why It Fails |
|---|---|
| Overly polished/produced content | TikTok algorithm penalizes "ad-looking" content. UGC aesthetic wins. |
| Posting once a week | Algorithm requires volume. 4-7× per week minimum for discovery. |
| Ignoring text overlays | 80%+ watch without sound. No text = no message. |
| Copying exact Instagram strategy to TikTok | Different algorithms, different audiences, different content expectations. |
| Buying followers/engagement | Destroys algorithm trust. Zero tolerance. |
