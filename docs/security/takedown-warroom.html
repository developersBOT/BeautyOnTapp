03

Do this first — preserve the evidence

Fake stores vanish the moment they're reported. Capture proof before you file. Use a device you don't mind exposing — never enter real personal or payment details.

  1. Screenshot the homepage with lovebeautyontapp.shop visible in the URL bar.
  2. Screenshot any logo, tagline, or product photos copied from your store — put your matching real page beside it. These are your infringement exhibits.
  3. Screenshot the checkout — especially the payment methods / card fields and any SA gateway logos (Payfast, Ozow, Payflex).
  4. Screenshot about / contact / returns / terms pages (usually missing, fake, or copied).
  5. Save the page as complete web page or print-to-PDF so the HTML + timestamp are preserved.
  6. Run the URL through urlscan.io and scamadviser.com and save the result links — independent corroboration.
  7. Pull the WHOIS: open lookup.icann.org, enter the domain, and record Registrar, Registrar Abuse Email, and Creation Date. Drop them into the templates below.

06

Proactive brand defense

These don't take the impostor down but blunt its impact and stop the next one.

  • Warn your customers now. Banner + social post: "The only official BeautyOnTApp store is beautyontapp.com and our official apps. We are not affiliated with lovebeautyontapp.shop — do not enter payment details there." This kills the scammer's conversion rate immediately.
  • Google Alerts for beautyontapp, "beauty on tapp", lovebeautyontapp to catch new clones early.
  • Register defensive domains you don't own (e.g. beautyontapp.shop and common typos) before squatters do.
  • Register the "BeautyOnTApp" trademark with CIPC if you haven't — it turns every future takedown into a one-click IP complaint and unlocks UDRP domain seizure.
  • Enable Meta Brand Rights Protection and lock in verified pages so Meta recognizes you as the rights holder.
  • Alert your payment providers (Payfast, Ozow, Payflex, Capitec Pay) that a fraudster is impersonating you — they blocklist brand-abusing merchants on their rails.
